Our new paper, **GeoStack: A Framework for Quasi-Abelian Knowledge Composition in VLMs**, is now available on arXiv. GeoStack allows for the modular stacking of domain-specific experts with constant-time inference complexity. Check out the **[project page](https://quantitativeimaginglaboratory.github.io/GeoStack/)** for more results and visualizations.
